The short version

Curb Appeal doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the game doesn't send anything over the internet.

What the game stores

Your companies (their names, colors and logos, your money and books, your team, gear and trucks, the places you've opened, your reviews and their pictures, your finds and achievements) and your settings are saved on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. On iPhone, iPad and Android, a copy of your save is also kept in the app's own storage on the device, so it survives if the system clears the game's web storage. None of it leaves your device, except as part of your device's own backups if you use them.

To wipe it, delete the app. To remove one company, open Companies from the main menu and tap the X on its card, then tap it again.

The names you type (your company's, and your team's if you rename them) stay on your device too. Nobody else sees them.

What the game reads on your device

The game reads your device's language setting to show its words in your language, and its clock to note when you last played each company (shown on its card). On a phone or tablet it can give a light vibration as you turn, when sound effects are on. If you connect a game controller, it reads the controller's buttons and sticks to let you play. None of this is recorded or sent anywhere.

Information we collect

None. Curb Appeal doesn't collect, sell or share personal information, and it doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising code.

Purchases

Curb Appeal has no purchases today. If a purchase is ever added, the App Store or Google Play will handle it, and we'll never see your name or payment details; this page will be updated first.

Children

Curb Appeal collects no information from anyone, children included.
