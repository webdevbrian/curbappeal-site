How do I play?

Start a New company from the main menu: give it a name, a color and a logo. Short tips at the bottom left walk you through your first day. The phone rings with jobs; take one, walk over to the lawn the arrow points to, mow it, trim the edges, sweep the clippings off the walk, and you're paid, with a review. As you grow, the phone opens up more: hiring, the hardware store, trucks, ads and new places.

The controls

Touch
Drag anywhere on the screen to walk, and let go to stop. Tap the green button (bottom right) to start or stop the mower; the small button beside it changes your tool. Pinch, or the + and - buttons on the left, to zoom.
Keyboard
W A S D or the arrow keys to move, Space to start the mower, Enter to carry on and Esc to go back. The mouse wheel, or + and -, to zoom.
Gamepad
The left stick or d-pad to move. A starts the mower, and carries on.

How do I mow straight stripes?

With Lanes steering (the default), a running mower stays in its lane, so a wobbly thumb can't bend a stripe. Push back the way you came to swing into the next lane, or sideways to turn a corner. Guide dots show the lane's edges ahead. If you'd rather steer exactly where you point, choose Free under Steering in Settings; Work guides turns the dots and the arrows off.

Where's everything else?

On your phone: the button at the top right. Jobs, reviews, the team, Hank's Hardware, the garage, the map, the radio, your finances, Settings, ending the day, and the main menu are all apps on it. A number on an app means there's something new in it.

How do I get to other places?

Buy the work truck on the phone's Market. Every place past Maple Street wants you to advertise first: run any ad from the phone's Ads app (it opens once you have a truck and a team). Then open the Map and drive there.

Can I skip to fall or winter?

Yes: the phone's Seasons app. Each season is ten days, and the next one comes round by itself, but you can pick one any time. Jobs that are on when you change it are called off.

Can it play by itself?

Yes: turn on Idle mode in Settings, and it takes the calls, walks over and does the work for you, at a little less pay. Steering, or a tap on the street or a button, takes over again; the Enable Idle checkbox on the screen starts it again.

There's no sound

On an iPhone or iPad, the game follows the silent switch, so turn the ringer on. On Android, turn up the media volume. Also check Music, Effects and Ringtone under Sound in Settings, and that the radio (top right) is switched on.

How do I change the language?

Settings → the globe button at the top left, or Language. The game speaks English, French, Italian, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Spanish, Korean, Turkish, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) and Russian, and starts in your device's language when it can. Picking one reloads the game in it.

The words are too small (or too big)

Settings → Text size makes the words and buttons bigger or smaller. Currency and Clock (12 or 24 hours) are there too.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, by itself every 20 seconds and whenever you leave the game. Continue on the main menu takes you back to where you left off, half-mown lawns and all.

How do I get back to the main menu?

The phone's Menu app. On Android, the back button closes whatever's open (the phone, a menu, a close-up), and on the main menu it puts the game in the background.

How do I start over?

Start a New company from the main menu: each company is its own save, pinned to the cork board under Companies. To remove one, tap the X on its card, then tap it again. This can't be undone. Your settings stay.
